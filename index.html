Before you buy

Straight answers.

Does "pay once" really mean forever?

Yes. One payment, and the tool is yours on any machine you own, for as long as you keep the file. Updates to the version you bought are free. If a future version is a genuinely new product, it will be priced as one — and your old one will keep working regardless.

Will it work with my model?

The spreadsheet tools attach to any model by pointing at cells you choose — you link inputs and outputs with ordinary formulas, so nothing about your model has to change. If it does not fit your file, ask before buying and you will get an honest answer.

Do my files leave my computer?

No. These tools run inside Excel or Sheets on your own data. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no account to create.

What are the system requirements?

Batch Runner for Excel needs desktop Excel on Windows (2016 or newer) and a macro-enabled file. It is not supported on Excel for Mac, Excel on the web, or mobile. Batch Runner for Sheets runs anywhere Google Sheets does, including on a Mac.

What if it does not help me?

Ask for a refund within 30 days and you will get one, without being asked to justify it. If instead you tell us what went wrong, that tends to end up in the next update.

Can I use it at work, or for client projects?

Yes. A single purchase covers one person, including commercial and client work. If several people at your company need it, there is a team licence — just get in touch.
